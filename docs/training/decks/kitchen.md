# Deck: Kitchen (Session 3, 45 min)

> Narration script — one line per slide. Exercise at the end is mandatory.

## Slide 1 — Your tool
- You live on the **Kitchen Display** (Dashboard → Kitchen Display).

## Slide 2 — The board
- Tickets grouped by state: `QUEUED → PREPARING → READY` → served.
- Each ticket: order number, items + quantities, special instructions, elapsed timer. Oldest first; overdue tickets highlight.

## Slide 3 — Working a ticket
- **Start** when cooking begins (this stamps the prep clock).
- **Ready** when plated — front of house sees it instantly.
- Transitions are one-way; you can't send a finished ticket back.

## Slide 4 — Prep-time indicators
- Elapsed time is colored against the restaurant's prep target (red = over target).
- A `live/capacity` load indicator warns before the kitchen is overloaded.

## Slide 5 — 86'ing an item
- Run out? **Menu → item → toggle Available off** — it vanishes from the shop immediately.
- Availability windows (e.g. brunch 9:00–11:30) flip automatically; you only manage the emergency switch.

## Slide 6 — Staff assignment
- Managers can assign you to a ticket; it shows as a chip on the card and clears at READY.

## Slide 7 — Your boundaries
- You can work the board and toggle availability — not prices, analytics, or staff (that's Manager/Admin).
