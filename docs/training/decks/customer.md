# Deck: Customer experience (Session 1, 30 min)

> Narration script — one line per slide. Exercise at the end is mandatory.

## Slide 1 — Welcome
- What we cover today: the guest-facing shop, end to end.

## Slide 2 — Getting in
- Public shop → **Register** (email + 8+ char password) or **Log in**.
- Forgot password → **Forgot password** emails a reset link.

## Slide 3 — The menu
- Photos, prices, descriptions; unavailable items are hidden or greyed out.
- **Favorites** (your reorders) and **Popular** (best-sellers you haven't tried).

## Slide 4 — Cart & checkout
- Add items, adjust quantities → **Checkout**.
- Final total is shown **before** you confirm.

## Slide 5 — Checkout options
- **Schedule** for later: 15 minutes to 30 days ahead.
- **Group order**: join with a 6-character code a friend shared.
- **Promo code**: entered in the promo box — invalid codes are rejected before payment.

## Slide 6 — Loyalty
- Every $1 actually paid = 1 point. 100 points = $1 off, capped at the order total.
- Balance + ledger on your **Account** page; points never expire while active.

## Slide 7 — Tracking
- **Orders** page shows live status: PENDING → CONFIRMED → PREPARING → READY → COMPLETED.
- The order page auto-refreshes — no reloads needed.

## Slide 8 — Reviews
- After completion: rate 1–5 + comment from the order page.
- The restaurant may reply publicly.

## Slide 9 — Your data
- Wrong order → contact the restaurant (they see your ticket by order number).
- Data deletion → GDPR erasure via the restaurant admin; privacy notice in the footer.
