# Customer Guide

You use the **shop** (the public site), not the dashboard.

## 1. Create an account
Go to **Register**. Use your email and a password of 8+ characters. Already
registered? **Log in**. Forgot your password? **Forgot password** emails you a
reset link.

## 2. Browse and order
- **Menu** shows every available dish with photo, price, and description.
  Items the kitchen has marked unavailable are hidden or greyed out.
- Add items to your cart, adjust quantities, then go to **Checkout**.
- At checkout you can:
  - **Schedule** the order for later (15 minutes to 30 days ahead).
  - **Join a group order** with a 6-character code a friend shared.
  - **Use a promo code** (enter it in the promo box — invalid codes are
    rejected before you pay).
  - **Redeem loyalty points** — 100 points = $1 off, capped at your order total.
- Pay by card. You'll see the final total before confirming.

## 3. Track your order
**Orders** lists your history with live status: `PENDING → CONFIRMED →
PREPARING → READY → COMPLETED`. The **order page** auto-refreshes — no need to
reload. Your name appears on the kitchen's display only after the restaurant
confirms.

## 4. Loyalty points
Every $1 you actually pay earns 1 point. See your balance and history on your
**Account** page. Points never expire while your account is active.

## 5. Reviews
After an order is completed you can leave a rating (1–5) and comment from the
order page. The restaurant can reply publicly.

## 6. Recommendations
The menu home shows **Favorites** (what you reorder most) and **Popular**
(best-sellers you haven't tried yet).

## Problems?
- Wrong order / missing item → contact the restaurant; they can see your full
  ticket by order number.
- Want your data deleted → the restaurant admin can process a GDPR erasure
  request; see the privacy notice linked in the site footer.
